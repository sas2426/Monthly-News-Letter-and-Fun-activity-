Segment 01 — SkyWatch Highlights

What to look up for this month

October delivers a planet at its best and brightest, plus two meteor showers on opposite ends of the lunar cycle — one in near-total darkness, one fighting moonlight.

OCT 4 · ALL NIGHT

Saturn at opposition

Saturn sits directly opposite the Sun, rising at sunset and visible all night at its closest, brightest point of the year. With rings opened to about 7.5°, this is the best telescope view of Saturn since 2023.

telescope recommended
OCT 8–9 · EVENING

Draconids peak — near-moonless

A modest shower (about 5–10/hr) but 2026 is a great year for it: the Moon is nearly new, and unlike most showers, the Draconids are best right after nightfall rather than after midnight. From South Asia, catch the evening of the 8th — the predicted peak falls near dawn locally.

no equipment needed
OCT 21–22 · PRE-DAWN

Orionids peak — Halley's debris

Earth passes through dust shed by Halley's Comet, producing fast meteors (66 km/s) radiating from Orion. A waxing gibbous Moon will wash out fainter ones this year — best after the Moon sets, in the hours before dawn.

moonlight will interfere
OCT 26

Full Hunter's Moon

October's full Moon, traditionally tied to preparing for winter, rises big and golden near the horizon — a good naked-eye and photography target the evening before peak illumination.

naked eye
Segment 02 — Aerospace & Missions

A record-fast launch, and India's SSLV goes commercial

October opens with back-to-back launches from Florida and continues with real milestones for India's space program.

NASA / SPACEX · OCT 1

🇺🇸Crew-13 docks in record time

Jessica Watkins (becoming the first NASA astronaut to fly twice on a SpaceX Dragon), pilot Luke Delaney, Roscosmos cosmonaut Sergey Teteryatnikov, and CSA's Joshua Kutryk launch aboard Dragon "Grace" — and dock at the ISS in under 8 hours, the fastest crewed U.S. spacecraft rendezvous ever.

SPACEX · OCT 1

🇺🇸Falcon Heavy's first NRO mission

Hours after Crew-13, a Falcon Heavy launches a classified reconnaissance payload for the U.S. National Reconnaissance Office — part of a SpaceX tripleheader of three launches in under 13 hours.

ISRO · OCT 15

🇮🇳GSLV Mk II lofts NVS-03

India's GSLV Mk II launches the NVS-03 navigation satellite to geosynchronous orbit, strengthening the NavIC regional navigation constellation.

ISRO · OCTOBER

🇮🇳SSLV's first commercial flight

ISRO's Small Satellite Launch Vehicle flies its first fully commercial mission this month — a milestone for India's push into the small-satellite launch market.

ISRO · LOOKING AHEAD

🇮🇳Gaganyaan-1 closes in on November

With hardware testing complete, India's uncrewed Gaganyaan-1 test flight — carrying humanoid robot Vyommitra — is now tracking toward a November launch window.

Activity

🌠 Two-shower meteor log

  • Watch 20 minutes on the evening of Oct 8 (Draconids) and 20 minutes predawn on Oct 21 (Orionids).
  • Count meteors in each session.
  • Compare: which shower actually produced more for you, despite the "official" rates?
Activity

🌕 Hunter's Moon photo

  • Photograph the Moon as it rises on Oct 25–26, when it looks huge near the horizon.
  • Take a second photo once it's high overhead.
  • Compare sizes — the "Moon illusion" means it's actually the same size both times.
